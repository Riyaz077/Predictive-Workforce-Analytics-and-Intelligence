# Responsible Use, Fairness and Privacy

TalentIQ is a portfolio demonstration, not an employment decision system.

- Never use predictions to hire, reject, fire, promote, compensate, discipline, or rank employees.
- Do not monitor individuals or infer sensitive personal characteristics.
- Synthetic records do not represent real workers and model metrics do not establish validity.
- Workforce analytics can reflect structural inequities or measurement bias.
- Any legitimate organizational research requires appropriate notice, legal basis, privacy safeguards, employee representation, fairness assessment, and human oversight.
- Prefer aggregate, voluntary, supportive workforce insights over individual-level risk scoring.
- Real deployment would require independent validation, governance, security review, and jurisdiction-specific legal assessment.
